How to Play

Controls

Flippers: Z / Left Shift / ← and / / Right Shift / →. The left button works every left flipper on every table, the right button every right flipper (8 in total!). On touch screens, tap the left or right half of the screen.

Plunger: hold Space (or hold the right side on touch), release to fire. A soft plunge into the flashing top lane is a Skill Shot.

Hero Power: E or tap your hero. Nudge: X / C / V (careful: TILT). Camera: V cycles Player / Fixed overview / Overhead.

The Quest

A monster stands in the Lair. Slay all five (Goblin, Skeleton, Cyclops, Mimic and the boss Azgaroth) to clear a floor. Deeper floors bring curses.

Each kill lights a Relic at the Vault. Quests (right panel) give rewards. Watch for Rat Swarms to squash, Rescue hurry-ups at the Vault, and Torch Frenzy.

Drop all four R-U-N-E targets to light Lock. Lock 3 balls at the Vault for Treasure Hoard Multiball.

The Other Tables

Dragon's Den (upper deck): every 2nd Shrine Ramp opens the lift. Wake the dragon, hatch its eggs, raid the hoard pit.

The Crypt (left table): complete H-E-R-O, then shoot the left orbit. Smash coffins and destroy the Lich for an extra ball.

The Forge (right table): complete S-L-A-Y, then shoot the right orbit. Every 4 anvil strikes forges a weapon: +1 damage for the rest of your game.
